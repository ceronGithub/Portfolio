My AI Philosophy: Clean Code, Not Just Code

I don't use AI to replace thinking; I use it to build better, faster, and cleaner. When I work with models like Claude, GPT-4, or Gemini, I don't just ask for a script and copy-paste it. I treat the AI as a pair-programmer that I direct to follow high standards. I focus on structured, "human-first" code. This means everything I build whether it's a Laravel backend or a C# desktop app is organized, readable, and easy for other developers to edit. For me, AI is about enhancing quality, not just quantity.

Structured Output

Every AI-generated piece follows my architecture rules modular, readable, no shortcuts.

Human-First Code

AI enhances quality, not just quantity. Other devs can read and edit everything I ship.

AI as Pair-Programmer

I direct the AI like a senior dev with context, constraints, and a source-of-truth doc.

ResortHub PH

📅 Mar 29, 2026 – Present 🛠️ Self-Built

A specialized booking ecosystem designed to professionalize the informal private resort industry in the Philippines. Functions as a high-trust intermediary between resort owners and seekers — eliminating "Ghost Resort" scams and uncoordinated calendar management. The core architecture uses a Hard Paywall & Information Masking strategy: sensitive property details (exact name, house number, owner contact) are programmatically hidden until a verified platform fee is confirmed via secure backend webhooks. A "7-Day Freshness" protocol requires owners to upload live in-app photos to stay visible in search results, and a Confirmation Pulse engine facilitates a real-time availability check — ensuring owners manually confirm a date before seekers can proceed to payment. Transforms the traditionally chaotic Facebook-based booking method into a secure, organized, professional digital experience.

💡 Tech highlights: Laravel 11 policy-based data masking, Supabase Realtime Broadcast for the 5-minute availability pulse, Row Level Security (RLS), and PayMongo Server-to-Server webhooks supporting GCash, Maya, and QRPh.
