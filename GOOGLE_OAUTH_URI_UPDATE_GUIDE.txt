================================================================
  MATTHEW STUDIO — Google OAuth URI Update Guide
  How to update Authorized JavaScript Origins &
  Authorized Redirect URIs in Google Cloud Console
================================================================

When you deploy your app to a live domain (e.g. https://matthewstudio.com),
you MUST add your production URLs to Google Cloud Console. This guide walks
you through the exact steps.

You do NOT remove the localhost entries — just ADD the new ones alongside them.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  WHEN DO YOU NEED THIS?
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - You deployed your app to Vercel, Railway, Render, or any host
  - You changed your domain name
  - localhost is working but production Google login is failing
  - You see error: "redirect_uri_mismatch" or "origin not allowed"

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 — Open Google Cloud Console
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open your browser and go to:
     https://console.cloud.google.com

  2. Make sure you are signed in with the same Google account
     you used to create the project (the one with the C icon).

  3. At the top left, confirm you are in the correct project:
     "My First Project"
     (or whichever project you created the OAuth client in)

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 — Navigate to Your OAuth Client
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. In the top search bar, type:
     "Credentials"
     and click the result under "APIs & Services"

  2. You will see the Credentials page.

  3. Under "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs", find:
     "Matthew Studio Drive"

  4. Click the pencil (edit) icon on the right side of that row.

  This opens the OAuth client editor.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 3 — Add Authorized JavaScript Origins
----------------------------------------------------------------

  This section controls which DOMAINS are allowed to make
  requests to Google. You need to add your live domain here.

  Current entries (do NOT delete these):
    http://localhost:3000

  What to do:
  1. Scroll down to the section titled:
     "Authorized JavaScript origins"

  2. Click the button:
     "+ Add URI"

  3. In the new input field that appears, type your live domain:
     https://yourdomain.com

     EXAMPLES:
       https://matthewstudio.com
       https://matthewstudio.vercel.app
       https://www.matthewstudio.com

  4. Do NOT include a trailing slash ( / ) at the end.
  4. Do NOT include any path after the domain.
     WRONG:  https://matthewstudio.com/admin
     RIGHT:  https://matthewstudio.com

  After adding, you should have TWO entries:
    http://localhost:3000
    https://yourdomain.com

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 4 — Add Authorized Redirect URIs
----------------------------------------------------------------

  This section controls where Google sends the user AFTER
  they approve access. Must match exactly what your app uses.

  Current entries (do NOT delete these):
    http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback

  What to do:
  1. Scroll down to the section titled:
     "Authorized redirect URIs"

  2. Click the button:
     "+ Add URI"

  3. In the new input field, type your live callback URL:
     https://yourdomain.com/api/auth/google/callback

     EXAMPLES:
       https://matthewstudio.com/api/auth/google/callback
       https://matthewstudio.vercel.app/api/auth/google/callback

  4. The path /api/auth/google/callback must be EXACT.
     This is the route in your Next.js app that handles
     the OAuth response from Google.

  After adding, you should have TWO entries:
    http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback
    https://yourdomain.com/api/auth/google/callback

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 5 — Save
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the page.

  2. Click the blue "Save" button.

  3. You will be redirected back to the Credentials list page.

  4. Google may take up to 5 minutes to apply the changes.
     If it still fails right away, wait 5 minutes and try again.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 6 — Update Your .env File on the Server
----------------------------------------------------------------

  After updating Google Console, also update your environment
  variables on your hosting platform (Vercel, Railway, etc.)

  Find the variable:
    GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI

  Change it from:
    http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback

  To:
    https://yourdomain.com/api/auth/google/callback

  On Vercel:
    1. Go to your project dashboard on vercel.com
    2. Click "Settings" → "Environment Variables"
    3. Find GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI → click Edit
    4. Update the value → Save
    5. Redeploy your app for the change to take effect

----------------------------------------------------------------
  TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------

  ERROR: "redirect_uri_mismatch"
  → The redirect URI in your .env does not match what Google
    has in the Authorized Redirect URIs list. Double-check
    both match exactly, including https:// and the path.

  ERROR: "origin not allowed"
  → Your domain is not in Authorized JavaScript Origins.
    Go back to Step 3 and add it.

  ERROR: "Access blocked: This app's request is invalid"
  → Your OAuth consent screen may not be published.
    Go to: Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services →
    OAuth consent screen → click "Publish App"

  Still failing after changes?
  → Wait 5–10 minutes. Google changes can take time to propagate.
  → Make sure you clicked Save in Step 5.
  → Make sure your hosting platform has the updated env variable.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  QUICK REFERENCE — All URLs at a Glance
----------------------------------------------------------------

  JAVASCRIPT ORIGINS:
    Local:       http://localhost:3000
    Production:  https://yourdomain.com

  REDIRECT URIs:
    Local:       http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback
    Production:  https://yourdomain.com/api/auth/google/callback

  ENV VARIABLE (GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI):
    Local:       http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback
    Production:  https://yourdomain.com/api/auth/google/callback
